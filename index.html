How to Play With Your Bubble

🖐️
Drag & Carry
Click or touch inside the bubble body to move it smoothly around the screen with realistic fluid inertia.
↔️
Stretch & Pull
Grab near the edge of the bubble and drag outward to deform it into elastic, teardrop or oblong shapes!
🧱
Squash on Edges
Flung or drag the bubble against screen borders to see it flatten and bulge against the walls like real liquid!
👉
Poke & Wobble
Click rapidly to poke inward indents and trigger harmonic jelly wobble waves across the membrane.
✨
Baby Bubbles
Poke the bubble repeatedly in quick succession to spawn floating miniature baby bubbles you can pop!
💥
The Big Pop!
Exceed the strain threshold or poke too many times, and the bubble will burst into shimmering droplets and respawn!
